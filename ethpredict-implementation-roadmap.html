04

Before another final test

Freeze code and source identity

Clean training revision, exact market/date/resolution and partition hashes, immutable input/split/config, units and coverage. The new lake does not reset the old test-use ledger.

Use genuinely held-out development evidence

Purged populations at both model levels, train-only transforms/calibration, same comparison rows and policy, complete attempt accounting including no-trade runs.

Demand economics and adequate coverage

Predeclare sufficient trades/blocks, positive aggregate net expectancy, block stability, 2x-cost survival and credible feature/noise evidence. Do not lower gates after seeing results.

Permit only a qualifying frozen policy

Use a fresh predeclared holdout once after validation selection. Reject missing evidence. More markets, larger grids, lower thresholds and leverage do not supply an edge.
